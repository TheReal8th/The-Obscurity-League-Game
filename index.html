7 Ways to Play

From a gentle warm-up to the toughest recall test in the game. Pick your level.

Medium
🎯

Classic

Pick an era, draw a random club, name the players — the more obscure, the more it's worth. Race to 500.

Hard
⚡

Blitz

Every club in an era, but only players under 20 games count. 60 or 90 seconds — no suggestions, no mercy.

Hardest
🏆

League Legend

One club, every era they've ever played. 10 minutes, but only 8 wrong guesses before it's over.

Daily
🔥

Daily Challenge

Same club for everyone, once a day. 5 minutes on the clock and one shared leaderboard to chase.

Hardest
👑

Rugby League God

One random club, 5 guesses total. Right or wrong, every guess costs a turn. Hit 300 or it's over.

Hard
🌏

RLWC Mode

9 World Cup nations, capped internationals since 1998. Only 5 wrong guesses across the whole game.

Medium
⚔️

State of Origin

Pick NSW or QLD and name anyone who's pulled on the jersey since 1998. One-gamers are worth the most.
