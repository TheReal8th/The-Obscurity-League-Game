9 Rugby League Modes

From a gentle warm-up to the toughest recall test in the game. Pick your level.

🧠

Ruck Trivia

No club to search — just a clue (club games, caps, Origin appearances) and a name to work out. 437 real clues, only 6 wrong guesses allowed.

🎯

Classic

Pick an era, draw a random club, name the players — the more obscure, the more it's worth. Race to 500.

New
📼

Super League Era

The 1995–97 Super League war. Draw a club-season, including the ones that no longer exist, and name the players.

⚡

Blitz

Every club in an era, but only players under 20 games count. 60 or 90 seconds — no suggestions, no mercy.

🏆

League Legend

One club, every era they've ever played. 10 minutes, but only 8 wrong guesses before it's over.

🔥

Daily Challenge

Same club and season for everyone, once a day — name the finals squad of, say, Penrith 2021. 5 minutes and one shared leaderboard.

👑

Rugby League God

One random club, 5 guesses total. Right or wrong, every guess costs a turn. Hit 300 or it's over.

🌏

RLWC Mode

9 World Cup nations, capped internationals since 1998. Only 5 wrong guesses across the whole game.

⚔️

State of Origin

Pick NSW or QLD and name anyone who's pulled on the jersey since 1998. One-gamers are worth the most.

🏏 Now Introducing Cricket

Flip the sport toggle in the game and name Big Bash and Australian Test players the same obscure-first way.

🏆

Big Bash Mode

One BBL club, 10 minutes, name anyone who's ever played for them. Plus Big Bash Classic and Blitz.

🎯

Test Match Classic

Australian Test players with 40 caps or fewer since 1998 — the more obscure, the more it's worth. Race to 500.

👑

Cricket God Mode

Pick Test, ODI or T20 and name Aussie batters from their runs and average. 5 turns to hit 300.

🔥

Historic

A real Test series — think the 2005 Ashes — and name everyone who played for one side. Tap again for the other side (England, then Australia).
